## Design specificity

The scene player is clearly made for this product: pixel classrooms, Vietnamese uniforms, a typewriter dialogue box, and consequences acted out. Everything around it is not. The landing is a generic explainer (hero, 2×2 stat cards, seven alternating bands). The result reads like a report card, and practice is a grid of six identical cards. The pixel look there is only fonts and notched borders. The best asset, the animated scene, never appears on the landing or the result.

The automated detector found no failing code patterns. One "bounce easing" warning is a false positive: it matched a character's jump animation. The browser scan found the contrast, line-length and heading problems listed below.

## What works

1. **The scene system.** It has consistent pixel scale, local detail, and acted-out consequences. That is the right teaching mechanic.
2. **The copy is humane and specific.** The "Bạn chọn / Cách mạnh hơn / một câu để nhớ" triad on the result is excellent, and habits describe behaviour, not identity.
3. **A restrained palette** of ink, paper, moss and mustard, with no gradients or glass. Resume, the reset confirmation and local-only data are handled well.

## Priority issues

1. **[P1] Unreadable text on the landing.**
   - **What:** In "Ba chế độ", the mode descriptions are near-white on light grey (1.1:1, app.css:293-298). In "Những điều trang này không làm", the paragraph is dark grey on black (1.7:1).
   - **Fix:** Correct the colours, and consider deleting "Ba chế độ", which repeats the "Vòng luyện" section.
2. **[P1] Pixel buttons break when stretched.**
   - **What:** On a 390px phone, "BẮT ĐẦU ĐÁNH GIÁ" becomes a black slab with a small green pill inside. The same happens with "LUYỆN 7 TÌNH HUỐNG…" on the result and with "Tình huống ngẫu nhiên" on practice.
   - **Fix:** The button's inner fill must stretch with its frame.
3. **[P1] The result page ends the game on a long report card.**
   - **What:** It is 7,000 to 10,600 px long. It opens with a big "77 / 100", the most test-like element in the product. The strength column has about 2,000 px of empty grey under it. Exercises and evidence repeat. Labels in "Điểm được tính thế nào?" run together ("TỪNG TÌNH HUỐNGMỗi…").
   - **Fix:** Make it a game debrief instead:
     - six skill bars;
     - one line each for "strongest" and "practise next";
     - one main button;
     - one replay of the evidence;
     - everything else folded away.
4. **[P1] The landing explains the game instead of showing it.**
   - **What:** The 5-step loop appears twice, along with the scoring formula and a disclaimer band. The only preview is a static text box.
   - **Fix:** Replace the stat grid with a live mini scene the visitor can tap to play, then cut the page down to hero, scene, a 6-skill strip and one privacy line.
5. **[P2] Scenes are not immersive on a phone.**
   - **What:** The site header and nav (about 240 px) plus a HUD that wraps onto two rows push the dialogue and choices below the fold.
   - **Fix:** Hide the site chrome while playing, use a single-row HUD, and pin the dialogue and choices to the bottom of the screen.

## Persona red flags

- **Linh (15, Android, one thumb):**
  - The main button looks broken.
  - The first screen after it is four paragraphs ending in "không phải thang đo tâm lý… chẩn đoán".
  - On the result she sees "77" first and will compare it with friends like a test score.
  - "11.5/18 điểm có trọng số" means nothing to her.
  - The practice button is about 3 screens down.
- **A parent or teacher:**
  - Nothing explains how "Cảnh EQ" differs from "Luyện EQ".
  - The section meant to explain the modes is the unreadable one.
  - The "Đánh giá lại" column links to the scenes, not to a reassessment.
- **A keyboard or screen-reader user:**
  - Nothing on screen mentions the shortcuts.
  - The headings jump from h1 to h3 on the result.
  - Skill codes are read aloud as letters.

## Minor

- VT323 is tiring for long Vietnamese text. Keep it for short UI and dialogue lines.
- The header sub-label "6 KỸ NĂNG · 20 TÌNH HUỐNG" is noise.
- The footer disclaimer repeats on every page, including inside the game.
- On the result, the accordion divider stops at 60% width, and the breadcrumb "Trang chủ · Đánh giá lại" is misleading.
- On practice, the skill cards have no tap affordance, and the "Không biết bắt đầu từ đâu?" box leaves half its width empty.
- The muted text (--ink-3 on --paper-2) is 4.4:1, just under AA. The formula and privacy lines run 124 to 130 characters wide.
- The "không phán xét / không chẩn đoán" disclaimer appears about 5 times and starts to sound defensive.
- Old screenshots in reports/ still show the "EQ HỌC ĐƯỜNG" brand.
